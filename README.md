# The MBA Playbook

Business school, sketched out.

**Live site:** [semiraayesufu.github.io/playbook](https://semiraayesufu.github.io/playbook/)

The MBA Playbook turns what business school teaches into tools you can use on real decisions. It started as my coursework notes from the Kelley School of Business, and it is now 81 tools across eight playbooks. It is free to use.

## The playbooks

| Playbook | The question |
|---|---|
| Applied AI | How do I use and build with AI? |
| Strategy | Where should we play? |
| Entrepreneurial Innovation | Is this idea worth building? |
| Finance & Accounting | Is it worth the money? |
| Marketing | Who buys, and why us? |
| Data & Research | Can I trust this finding? |
| B2B Marketing | How do businesses buy? |
| Negotiation & Persuasion | How do I get to yes? |

## How each tool works
- **What it is**, in simple terms, and why it matters.
- **Steps** you can follow in order, with the formula where there is one.
- **In practice**, a worked example with the numbers laid out line by line. Each playbook follows one example company from tool to tool, so the figures connect.
- **Watch out**, the mistake people make most often.

## Also on the site

- **Hand-drawn maps.** Each playbook opens with a sketch of its stages. Click a tool to jump to it, and follow the loop arrows back to the step you revisit.
- **Templates.** Eight spreadsheets with the formulas already built. Replace the example figures with your own.
- **Glossary.** 140 business terms, defined simply.
- **Search.** Find any tool or term from the top of the page.

## How it is built

The whole site is one HTML file with no build step. The sketches are drawn with [rough.js](https://roughjs.com).

## Credits

The tools draw on frameworks developed by many people, from Michael Porter's Five Forces to Robert Cialdini's principles of persuasion. The full list is on the site's About page. Example companies are fictional, and real company names appear only as public illustrations.
